Layer 1 is the dominant bottleneck — and it's invisible
The VWAP band check at the end of L1 has no log line at all — it just returns None. Out of 560 scans, only 15 generated a loggable L1 signal. The rest died quietly for one of these reasons (all DEBUG or silent):

Price too far from VWAP (silent return None) ← the main culprit
EMA = "neutral" (silent)
EMA consecutive bars not confirmed (DEBUG)
EMA slope decelerating (DEBUG)
Intraday session bias (DEBUG)
Price on wrong VWAP side (DEBUG)

The VWAP_BAND_PCT=0.0075 means price must be within 0.75% of VWAP. On a trending day like today, most stocks are either well above or well below VWAP for long stretches — within 0.75% is a narrow window that only opens briefly.

Layer 3 is the second bottleneck
L3 requires a 1-min candle that is simultaneously green (close > open) AND rising (close > prev close). Both conditions must be true. This is a strict double requirement — a flat or mildly red candle kills an otherwise valid setup even when the stock is clearly trending. 9 out of 15 signals got killed here.

Where to focus, in order of impact:
1. VWAP band — highest leverage, safest change
Going back from 0.75% toward the old 0.01 (1.0%) would roughly 2× the L1 pass rate. The setup quality doesn't meaningfully degrade — you're still requiring a VWAP pullback, just with a slightly more forgiving window. On a $200 stock, 0.75% is ±$1.50; 1.0% is ±$2.00. Either is still a "near VWAP" entry.
2. L3 momentum — second highest impact
Drop the double requirement: keep "rising close > prev close" but remove the "green candle (close > open)" requirement. A 1-min bar can be slightly red (close just below open) while still closing higher than the previous bar — that's still upward momentum. The current logic sometimes fires one scan cycle late, after the momentum candle has already passed, which means missed entries.
3. Leave L5 alone
The regime gate is working correctly. MRVL and SOFI CALL signals while SPY was bearish should be blocked — those would likely have been bad trades today.
4. L4/L6 are not the problem
Zero blocks today. They're not overfitting or over-filtering — they're simply not being triggered.
Summary: VWAP band is the invisible 97% filter. Loosen that first and watch how many more L1 signals start appearing in the log. Then reassess L3.

========== Trend Reversal =======

TREND_REVERSAL — How It Works (S1 / vwap_pullback)

What it does: Closes 100% of a position when the 15-minute EMA (period 21) flips direction against the trade — bullish EMA on a PUT, bearish EMA on a CALL.
Data source: 15-min completed bars, fetched with TREND_LOOKBACK_DAYS (default 5 trading days) of history so the EMA has enough bars to be meaningful.
Guard A — minimum hold window (TREND_REVERSAL_MIN_HOLD_MINUTES, default 10 min): Suppression applies only if the trade is profitable (current option price ≥ entry price). If the trade is already losing, TREND_REVERSAL can fire immediately regardless of how long the trade has been open.
Guard B — consecutive-bar confirmation (TREND_REVERSAL_CONFIRM_BARS, default 1): The EMA must have been in the adverse direction for at least 1 completed 15-min bar before exit fires. Prevents reacting to a momentary tick.
Guard C — VWAP confirm: Was removed. Originally suppressed TREND_REVERSAL when the underlying was still above (for CALLs) or below (for PUTs) the entry VWAP. In practice it blocked almost all TREND_REVERSAL exits because we enter near VWAP — the underlying is usually still at/near entry VWAP even as the option loses value. VWAP_BREAK handles the VWAP-cross exit separately.
Cooldown after exit (TREND_REVERSAL_COOLDOWN_MINUTES, default 60 min): After a TREND_REVERSAL exit, the symbol enters the same cooldown as STOP and VWAP_BREAK. No re-entry for 60 minutes.
Known limitation: TREND_REVERSAL compares the current EMA direction against the trade direction — it has no memory of what the EMA looked like at entry. If a trade was entered while the EMA was already adverse (entry quality failure), TREND_REVERSAL reads it the same as a genuine post-entry reversal and fires as soon as Guard A/B are satisfied.
Current status: DISABLED. Removed from check_exit_conditions as of June 2026 while system is under manual observation. Config keys (TREND_REVERSAL_*) remain in .env for easy re-enablement.
